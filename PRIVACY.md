# Notification Sync privacy policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

Notification Sync shows your Android phone's notifications on a Pebble watch and lets you act on them from the watch.

## What the app reads

With your permission (Android's Notification Access), the app reads the notifications on your phone: the app that
posted each one, its title, sender, message text, attached pictures, and the actions it offers (such as Reply or
Mark as read). It also reads the list of installed apps so you can set rules per app.

## Where it goes

- Notifications are sent **only to your own Pebble watch**, over Bluetooth, through the Pebble app on your phone.
- The app has **no internet permission**. Nothing is uploaded, sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising or
  analytics.
- Your settings and a short notification history stay on your phone, in the app's private storage, and are deleted
  when you uninstall the app.
- Diagnostic logs are kept on your phone. They only leave it if you tap **Tools → Save logs** and choose where to
  send them yourself.

## Your choices

You can turn off Notification Access at any time in Android's settings, hide any app from the watch inside
Notification Sync, or uninstall the app to delete all of its data.
